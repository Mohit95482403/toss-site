Rules & Guide

How TossArena Works

Everything you need to know about navigating matches, utilizing complimentary credits, and making toss predictions.

1

What is TossArena?

TossArena is a web platform designed exclusively around cricket match coin toss strategy. In cricket, the coin toss dictates pitch advantage, bowling order, and weather strategy. TossArena allows enthusiasts to study match data and place predictions without financial risk.

2

Exploring Cricket Fixtures

Navigate to the Matches page to browse fixtures scheduled across domestic and international cricket. Each match card displays scheduled UTC times (rendered in your local browser timezone), venue information, and countdown cutoffs.

3

The Toss Prediction Process

When live prediction placement opens in upcoming implementation phases, users choose:

  • 🪙 Toss Winner: Which team captain will call or win the coin flip.
  • 🏏 Toss Decision: Whether the winning captain will choose to Bat or Bowl first.
  • ⏱️ Cutoff Lock: Predictions close automatically before the official toss takes place.
4

What are Platform Credits?

Credits are platform tokens provided to all registered accounts (1,000 starting credits). They exist purely for prediction gameplay and tracking. They cannot be purchased, deposited with real currency, cashed out, or redeemed for monetary value.
